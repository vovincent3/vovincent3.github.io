DJ Setup Guides

Simple guides for building a clean and reliable DJ setup.

Beginner DJ Setup Checklist

  • DJ controller or all-in-one system
  • Laptop or USB music library depending on your gear
  • DJ headphones
  • Laptop stand
  • XLR cables, RCA cables, and USB cables
  • Power strip and extension cord
  • Backup adapters
  • Carrying case or backpack

Mobile DJ Cable Guide

For private events, always ask the venue what input their speaker system uses. Many professional systems use XLR. Smaller speakers may use RCA, quarter-inch, or combo inputs. Carrying backup cables can save your set if the venue setup is different than expected.

What to Upgrade First

If your setup is basic, upgrade in this order: headphones, cables, laptop stand, controller, then speakers. Good cables and reliable headphones matter more than people think because they prevent avoidable problems during a set.
